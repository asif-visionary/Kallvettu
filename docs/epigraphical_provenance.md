### 1. Mangulam Rock Inscription (மாங்குளம் தமிழ் பிராமி கல்வெட்டு)
* **Catalog ID**: `mangulam_brahmi`
* **Corpus Citation**: 
  * Iravatham Mahadevan, *Early Tamil Epigraphy: From the Earliest Times to the Sixth Century A.D.* (Harvard Oriental Series, Vol. 62 / Cre-A, 2003), Inscription #1, pp. 317–324.
  * K.V. Raman, *Some Aspects of Pandyan History in the Light of Recent Discoveries* (Madras University, 1971).
  * *Annual Report on Indian Epigraphy (ARE)* 1906, No. 460.
* **Geographical Location**: Mangulam (Meenakshipuram), Ovali Hill (Kalugumalai cave), Madurai District, Tamil Nadu (9.986° N, 78.237° E).
* **Dynasty & Ruler**: Early Pandya Dynasty — Nedunjeliyan I (Sangam King *Ariyappadaikadantha Nedunjeliyan* / *Kadalan Vazhuthi*).
* **Regnal Era**: c. 3rd–2nd Century BCE.
* **Script**: Early Tamil-Brahmi (Damili / Southern Brahmi).
* **Language**: Classical Old Tamil (சங்கத் தமிழ் / தொல் தமிழ்).
* **Epigraphical Summary**: Records the chiseled creation of stone beds (*pāḷiy*) in a rock shelter for the Jain ascetic Kaninatha Nanda Asiriyar by Kadalan Vazhuthi, an officer (*ikuvaṉ*) under the Sangam Pandya monarch Nedunjeliyan.
* **Diplomatic Text**:
  ```text
  கணிய் நந்தஆஸிரிய் இகுவன் கட்அளன் வழுதிஇ கொடிபித பாளிய்
  ```

### 2. Pugalur Rock Inscription (புகழூர் சேரர் கல்வெட்டு)
* **Catalog ID**: `pugalur_chera`
* **Corpus Citation**:
  * Iravatham Mahadevan, *Early Tamil Epigraphy*, 2003, Inscription #56, pp. 407–418.
  * R. Nagaswamy, "Chera Inscriptions of Pugalur", in *Proceedings of the First International Conference-Seminar of Tamil Studies* (Kuala Lumpur: IATR, 1968), Vol. 1, pp. 415–420.
  * *Epigraphia Indica*, Vol. XXXVII (1967–68), pp. 287–292.
* **Geographical Location**: Aruattarmalai, Velayuthampalayam, near Pugalur, Karur District, Tamil Nadu (11.065° N, 78.012° E).
* **Dynasty & Ruler**: Sangam Chera Dynasty (Karur Irumporai line) — King Ko Athan Cheral Irumporai, his son Perunkadungo, and grandson Ilankadungo.
* **Regnal Era**: c. 1st–2nd Century CE.
* **Script**: Tamil-Brahmi (Middle Period).
* **Language**: Classical Old Tamil.
* **Epigraphical Summary**: Records the cutting of rock beds for the Jain ascetic Cenkāyapan by Crown Prince Ilankadungo on his investiture as heir apparent, validating three generations of Chera kings mentioned in the Sangam anthology *Pathitrupathu*.
* **Diplomatic Text**:
  ```text
  கோ ஆதன் செல்லிரும்பொறை மகன் பெருங்கடுங்கோ மகன் இளங்கடுங்கோ இளங்கோ ஆக அறுத்த கல்
  ```

### 3. Pulankurichi Rock Inscription (பூலாங்குறிச்சி வட்டெழுத்துக் கல்வெட்டு)
* **Catalog ID**: `pulankurichi_vatteluttu`
* **Corpus Citation**:
  * Y. Subbarayalu and M.R. Raghava Varier, "Pulankurichi Inscriptions: A Re-examination", *Avanam* (Journal of the Tamil Nadu Archaeological Society), Vol. 1 (1991), pp. 11–28.
  * R. Poongundran, *Kalvettiyal: Sila Varalatru Kurippugal* (Chennai: Pavai Publications, 2000).
  * P.R. Srinivasan, "Pulankurichi Inscriptions", *Epigraphia Indica*, Vol. XXXIX (1971), pp. 191–198.
* **Geographical Location**: Pulankurichi Hill, Tiruppattur Taluk, Sivaganga District, Tamil Nadu (10.158° N, 78.643° E).
* **Dynasty & Ruler**: King Chendan Kurran (Post-Sangam Kalabhra transitional era).
* **Regnal Era**: 27th Regnal Year (c. late 5th Century CE, astronomical date: 498 CE).
* **Script**: Early Vatteluttu (வட்டெழுத்து).
* **Language**: Early Middle Tamil.
* **Epigraphical Summary**: One of the most monumental rock-cut edicts in South India (~15 meters across), establishing institutional land administration terms (*brahmadeya*, *devadana*, *pallichandam*) and a fortified trade center (*kādigai-tāvāḷam*).
* **Diplomatic Text**:
  ```text
  கோச்சேந்தன் கூற்றற்கு யாண்டு இருபத்தேழாவது கடிகைத்தாவளத்து ஊராரும்
  ```

### 4. Chengam Hero Stone Inscription (செங்கம் நடுகல் கல்வெட்டு)
* **Catalog ID**: `hero_stone_nadukal`
* **Corpus Citation**:
  * R. Nagaswamy (General Editor), *Chengam Nadukarkal* (செங்கம் நடுகற்கள்), Special Epigraphical Series, Department of Archaeology, Government of Tamil Nadu, Chennai, 1974.
  * *South Indian Inscriptions*, Vol. XXXIII (Hero Stones of North Arcot).
* **Geographical Location**: Motakkal / Chengam Taluk, Tiruvannamalai District, Tamil Nadu (12.308° N, 78.902° E).
* **Dynasty & Ruler**: Pallava Dynasty (Reign of Simhavishnu / Mahendravarman I border conflicts with Western Gangas).
* **Regnal Era**: c. 6th–7th Century CE.
* **Script**: Archaic Vatteluttu.
* **Language**: Classical Tamil with folk hero-ballad idiom.
* **Epigraphical Summary**: Commemorates warrior Sathan who fell in combat (*pattaṉ*) defending the village cattle herd (*thoru*) from enemy plunderers (*thoru-p-poosal*), demonstrating the living antiquity of Sangam *Karanthai Thinai* warrior ethics.
* **Diplomatic Text**:
  ```text
  தொறுக்கொண்டு போகிய பூசலுள் நின்றெறிந்து பட்டான் சாத்தன் நடுகல்
  ```

* **Catalog ID**: `thanjavur_rajaraja`
* **Corpus Citation**:

* **Geographical Location**: Vimana plinth base (Southern Wall), Brihadisvara Great Temple, Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu (10.783° N, 79.132° E).

* **Diplomatic Text**:

## 3. Epigraphical Text Representation Standard (Five-Tier Model)

Following modern computational epigraphy methodology, Kallvettu represents inscriptions in five strictly segregated academic tiers:

1. **Tier 1 (Diplomatic Transcript)**: Strict unnormalized representation preserving original graphemes, epigraphical orthography, missing pullis, archaic ligatures, and damaged lacunae represented inside brackets `[...]`.
2. **Tier 2 (Normalized Unicode)**: Standard modern Tamil Unicode script with explicit pullis restored, Grantha glyphs normalized, and epigraphical abbreviations expanded.
3. **Tier 3 (Word-Segmented Morphological Text)**: Decomposed sandhi compounds with case markers, clitics, and particles separated by word boundaries.
4. **Tier 4 (Vernacular Modern Tamil Translation)**: Accurate grammatical interpretation in contemporary literary Tamil.
5. **Tier 5 (Scholarly English Translation)**: Academic translation with historical, dynastic, and sociopolitical annotations.

## 5. Multi-Engine Verification & AI Epigraphical Provenance Protocol

To ensure academic and scientific integrity, Kallvettu operates under an explicit provenance protocol:

### 1. Zero Machine Hallucination & Evidence Stratification
- **Ground Truth Corpus**: Primary lithic squeezes documented in official ASI and Epigraphia Indica publications.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Consensus**: Multi-engine OCR models (PaddleOCR, BHASHINI, Sarvam Vision, Gemini) propose readings; disagreements are explicitly flagged as *Disputed Tokens* for researcher verification rather than resolved through blind automated majority voting.
- **Model Limitation Transparency**: Modern Indic OCR models trained on contemporary print/signage Tamil are explicitly demarcated as unsuited for 3rd c. BCE Brahmi/Damili ligatures without epigraphist review.

### 2. ISO 15919 Transliteration Schema
Academic romanization follows the ISO 15919 standard for South Asian scripts with strict diacritical representation:
- Long vowels: *ā, ī, ū, ē, ō*
- Retroflex consonants: *ṭ, ṇ, ḷ, ḻ*
- Alveolar consonants: *ṟ, ṉ*
- Velar & Palatal nasals: *ṅ, ñ*

### 3. Epigraphical Named Entity Recognition (NER) Taxonomy
Entities extracted from stone charters are classified into 7 historical categories:
1. `Historical Ruler / King (மன்னன்)`: e.g. *இராஜராஜ, நெடுஞ்செழியன், சேந்தன்*
2. `Dynasty / Royal Lineage (அரச மரபு)`: e.g. *சோழர், பாண்டியர், சேரர், பல்லவர்*
3. `Sacred Temple / Monastic Shrine (திருக்கோயில் / பாளி)`: e.g. *கற்றளி, பெருங்கோயில், பாளி*
4. `Tax-Free Land Grant (அறக்கொடை நிலம்)`: e.g. *பிரம்மதேயம், பள்ளிச்சந்தம், தேவதானம், இறையிலி*
5. `Democratic & Administrative Institution (நிர்வாக அவை)`: e.g. *குடவோலை, வாரியம், அதிகாரி, சேனாபதி*
6. `Hero Stone & Martial Event (நடுகல் / வீரச்செய்தி)`: e.g. *நடுகல், தொறுகோள், பூசல், பட்டான்*
7. `Measurement & Weight Unit (அளவை / எடை)`: e.g. *கழஞ்சு, காசு, வேலி, மா, மரக்கால்*
